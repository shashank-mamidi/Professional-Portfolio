About

About Me

I am a Senior Analog Design Engineer with experience across the full analog and mixed-signal IC design lifecycle. My work involves translating system-level requirements into reliable transistor-level circuit solutions that meet demanding targets for performance, power, area, reliability, and manufacturability.

My technical background includes circuit architecture, schematic design, simulation, PVT and Monte Carlo verification, layout collaboration, post-layout extraction analysis, silicon bring-up, bench characterization, and simulation-to-silicon correlation.

I enjoy solving complex circuit design challenges and optimizing designs for real-world silicon behavior. My approach combines strong analog fundamentals, disciplined simulation methodology, layout awareness, and cross-functional collaboration.
